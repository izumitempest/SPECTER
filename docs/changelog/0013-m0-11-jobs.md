## Summary

Jobs now exist as database rows. `jobs/manager.py` implements submit /
start / finish / fail / pause_disk with the per-image lock: at most one
active carve-or-hash job per image; concurrent submissions queue by default,
or raise `JobLockError` when the caller refuses to queue (both paths are
explicit and auditable). `paused_disk` deliberately does not stamp
`finished_at` — a paused job is resumed, not completed. `jobs/pool.py` is a
thin, lazy ProcessPoolExecutor factory with a `map_parallel` helper, and its
docstring pins the E-06 rule: the carve scan loop is sequential in one
process and never goes through the pool.
